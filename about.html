Who is Ray?

Ray Schneider is a full-spectrum engineering leader with over 20 years of experience spanning startups and Fortune 50 organizations. He combines hands-on software development expertise with enterprise architecture, engineering management, and security engineering—equally effective writing code, advising executives, or leading teams of up to 50 people.

Throughout his career, Ray has delivered hybrid cloud infrastructure scaling to over one million vCPUs and 30+ petabytes of storage, built AI-enabled network security and threat detection products from concept to market, and designed enterprise-wide architecture modernization programs that reduced technical debt and total cost of ownership by tens of millions annually. He has founded open-source security projects with international partners, created self-service infrastructure platforms replacing legacy proprietary systems, and built real-time executive analytics dashboards powering business intelligence at scale. His work spans distributed systems, cybersecurity tooling, identity and access management, network packet capture at multi-gigabit line rates, and local AI-assisted development for autonomous systems.

As a technical leader, Ray owns full-stack implementation across backend, frontend, APIs, and infrastructure while making explicit speed-versus-maintainability trade-offs. He transforms ambiguous goals into clear engineering solutions and production-ready MVPs built on foundations that scale past initial launch. As a people leader, he has hired, mentored, and performance-managed architecture directors, senior tech leads, and engineering managers—improving retention, bench strength, and succession planning. He chairs architecture review boards, establishes governance standards organization-wide, and balances cost, risk, security, and delivery velocity.

Ray is recognized for aligning technical strategy to business outcomes, mentoring senior technical leaders, and improving security, quality, and cost efficiency at scale. A U.S. Marine Corps veteran, his technical stack spans Linux, Python, Go, Rust, C/C++, OpenStack, Kubernetes, and local AI/SLM development.
