Core Values

November 23, 2026

For many years I have shared the following Core Values with teams and places I have worked. I figured why not share them here.

Now to be quite honest, I do not recall any longer if I built these on top of something else, or if I just generated these from my own thoughts. So any semblance to others is a happy accident of like mindedness. Over the years, I have modified the wording a bit. I'd be curious to hear about any additions or thoughts about these people may have.

The following is the current set:

Core Values

People

  • Respect & care for well-being & professional development
  • Maintain an atmosphere comprised of trust, empowerment, teamwork
  • Get the best people in the right place, including yourself

Courage

  • Take risk when necessary
  • Listen to our critics
  • Respond to the challenge and opportunity presented as it presents

Education

  • Its not about collecting degrees or certifications
  • Recognize that life-long learning is a necessity
  • Provide and/or seek opportunity for further learning

Empowerment

  • Provide tools and information needed to create opportunity
  • Enable the decision maker, the innovator, the doer

Teamwork

  • Work together with common purpose, goals
  • Create a culture and atmosphere where everyone has a chance to contribute
  • Achieve success as a team while we strive towards mission delivery

Integrity

  • Say what you believe, do what you say! Be WYSIWYG!
  • Work with trust, honesty, caring, fairness, loyalty and diversity

Attitude

  • Reflects these values
  • Be enthusiastic, proactive and passionate
  • Keep that PMA (Positive Mental Attitude)

Accountability

  • Being accountable earns the right to hold others accountable.
  • Be a Driver NOT a Rider. Own it. Deliver success.

Quality

  • Expect nothing but the best from you, your team and others around you
  • All of the above are necessary to achieve quality.
